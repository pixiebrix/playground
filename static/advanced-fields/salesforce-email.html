Salesforce-style CKEditor4 email composer

Reproduces the frame split of the Salesforce Classic email composer for issue #10379 Phase 2: the CKEditor 4 editable body is a nested about:blank frame inside an /email/htmlbody iframe, while the Send button is here in the top frame. No single frame owns both the editor and the button, so a same-frame guard can't block the send — the extension mirrors the body's verdict up and blocks at this button. 🔙 Visit Advanced fields.
